Notifications Guide

Set up smart reminders to protect your streaks

1
Enable Browser Permission
Go to Settings → Notification Permission and click "Allow". A browser popup will appear — select Allow to grant access. Without this step, no notifications will be sent.
2
Toggle Daily Reminder On
Enable the Daily Reminder toggle in Settings. This activates a browser notification at your chosen time every day, nudging you to check in on your habits.
3
Choose Your Reminder Time
A Reminder Time picker will appear — set it to a time you're reliably available. Morning (e.g. 7:30 AM) or lunchtime works great for most people. The notification fires daily at this exact time.
4
Enable Streak Nudge (Bonus!)
Turn on Streak Nudge for a late-evening reminder after 8 PM if you still have incomplete habits. This is especially powerful for protecting multi-day streaks before midnight!
💡 Important: Notifications require HabitFlow to be open in an active browser tab. They use the Web Notifications API — a native browser feature that works without any install. If notifications aren't showing, check your OS notification settings and ensure the browser isn't in "Do Not Disturb" mode.
